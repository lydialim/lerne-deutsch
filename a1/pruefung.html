Prüfung (the exam)

What happens in each part of the Goethe-Zertifikat A1, with model answers.

23 · Goethe-Zertifikat A1 (the A1 exam)

The exam has four parts. You pass with 60 of 100 points (sechzig ZEKH-tsikh von hundert HOON-dert). Check the official Modellsatz (sample test) from the Goethe-Institut before the exam.

Teil (part)Dauer (time)Was? (what?)
Hören (listening)ca. 20 Min.Announcements, phone messages, short dialogues. Listen for numbers, times, prices, days.
Lesen (reading)ca. 25 Min.Short emails, signs, notices, ads.
Schreiben (writing)ca. 20 Min.Teil 1: fill in a form. Teil 2: a short message, about 30 words.
Sprechen (speaking)ca. 15 Min., in a groupTeil 1: introduce yourself. Teil 2: ask and answer questions. Teil 3: make requests and react.

Sprechen · Teil 2

Fragen stellen und antworten (ask and answer)

You get a topic (Thema) and a card with one word. Make a question with that word for your partner. Then answer their question.

Reminder · du-questions. With your partner use du: the verb ends in -st: machst du, gehst du, kaufst du. Irregular: isst du (essen), fährst du (fahren).
Sprechen · Teil 3

Bitten formulieren (make requests)

You get a picture card (for example a window or a pen). Ask your partner for something, and your partner reacts.

Bild (picture)Bitte (request)Reaktion (reaction)
Fenster (window)Kannst du bitte das Fenster öffnen? (Can you please open the window?)Ja, gern. (Yes, gladly.)
Kuli (pen)Hast du einen Kuli für mich? (Do you have a pen for me?)Ja, hier bitte. (Yes, here you are.)
KaffeeIch möchte bitte einen Kaffee. (I would like a coffee, please.)Gern. Mit Milch? (Sure. With milk?)
Wasser (water)Kannst du mir bitte das Wasser geben? (Can you give me the water, please?)Hier, bitte. (Here you are.)
Handy (phone)Darf ich dein Handy benutzen? (May I use your phone?)Nein, tut mir leid. (No, sorry.)
Reminder · Infinitive at the end. Kannst du bitte das Fenster öffnen? kann/möchte/darf comes early, the second verb goes to the end.
Reminder · Akkusativ: only der changes. After haben, möchten, brauchen: der → den, ein → einen. Ich möchte einen Kaffee. die, das and plural stay the same: eine Banane, ein Brot.

Hören und Lesen üben (practising listening and reading)

In the Goethe sample test (Modellsatz) both parts have three sections. You never need to understand every word: you only hunt for one piece of information (a time, a price, a day, a place).

TeilHören (listening, about 20 min)Lesen (reading, about 25 min)
1Short everyday dialogues. You choose a, b or c.Short notes or e-mails. You decide richtig / falsch (true / false).
2Announcements (station, shop, airport).Ads or web pages. You decide which one fits the situation.
3Phone messages.Signs and notices.

The exact question types and how many times you hear each text can change. Check the current official Modellsatz on goethe.de before your exam.

Hören: 4 stepsworks for every listening task
  1. Read the question first. Underline the W-word: Wann? Wo? Wie viel? Now you know what to hunt for.
  2. Say the possible answers to yourself in German: 7:30 = halb acht (halp akht).
  3. Listen for traps: nicht … sondern (not … but) and nicht am Montag, sondern am Mittwoch. The second one is the answer!
  4. Second listening: only check. If unsure, guess: there are no minus points.
Lesen: look, don't readscan for the key word
  1. Read the statement first, then find the same key word in the text (a day, a time, a number).
  2. Compare the details: Montag ≠ Mittwoch, 12 ≠ 20, offen (open) ≠ geschlossen (closed).
  3. Watch the little words that change everything: nicht, kein, nur (only), ab (from), bis (until).
  4. Opening hours: Mo – Fr = Monday to Friday, geschlossen = closed, geöffnet = open.
Reminder · The trap words in the exam. nicht … sondern (not … but), leider (unfortunately: a plan changes), später / früher (later / earlier), Verspätung (delay), statt (instead of). When you hear one of these, the answer usually comes right after it.

✎ Schreiben üben (writing practice with a checker)

Pick a task, write about 30 words, then press Prüfen. The checker looks for what the examiner counts: greeting, closing, all three points, length, plus capital letters and du / Sie. It does not check grammar, so also compare with the model answer. Your text is saved in this browser only.

🎙 Sprechen üben (speaking practice)

Speaking is the part most learners skip, and the part you can practise alone. Listen to a model sentence, record yourself, then compare. Your recordings stay on this device: nothing is uploaded.

🔊 🎙 Audio einrichten (set up audio: iPhone or computer)

So funktioniert es (how it works): The German is read aloud by a voice built into your browser or phone. No audio files are downloaded and no account is needed. Your recordings are made in your browser and are not uploaded. They are kept only until you reload or close the page.

iPhone

  1. Open the site in Safari or Chrome. Turn the volume up.
  2. Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Deutsch. Download a German voice such as Anna or Markus. Enhanced or Premium voices sound more natural.
  3. Recording: when you first tap 🎙 Aufnehmen, tap Allow for the microphone. If you said no, go to Settings → Safari → Microphone, or use the aA button in the address bar, then reload.

Computer (Windows or Mac)

  1. Use Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
  2. Install a German voice. Windows: Settings → Time & language → Language & region → Deutsch → Language options → speech voice. Mac: System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → System Voice → Manage Voices → Deutsch. The menu names differ between Windows versions.
  3. Recording: when the browser asks, click Allow. If you blocked it, click the padlock in the address bar, set Microphone to Allow, then reload.

In der App (in the app)

  • 🔊 Stimme & Tempo at the top of each page: choose a German voice, press Test, and tick langsam for slower speech. Your choice is saved in this browser.
  • Every 🔊 button reads its sentence or table cell aloud with the same voice.
  • Anhören plays the model sentence, Langsam plays it slower, and Aufnehmen records up to 25 seconds. Vergleichen plays the model, then your recording.
  • If you hear English instead of German, no German voice is installed on this device. Follow the steps above.

Lerntipps (study tips)

Small habits that help the rules stick.

  • Colour sticky notes at home: blue for der, red for die, green for das. Stick them on things: der Tisch, die Lampe, das Bett.
  • Three rooms in your head: der nouns live in a blue room, die nouns in a red room, das nouns in a green room. When you learn a noun, picture it in its room.
  • Say the noun package aloud three times: der Tisch, die Tische. Article + noun + plural, always together.
  • Numbers everywhere: read prices in the supermarket, bus numbers and house numbers in German, in your head.
  • Spell your name and street once a day: M – A – R – I – A (emm – ah – err – ee – ah).
