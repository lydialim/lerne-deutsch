Teil C

Themen (topics)

Ready-made sentences about you, your work, your free time, finding your way, food, family, your flat and countries. Change them to fit your life.

10 · Über mich (about me)

The most important text you will ever say in German. In the exam (Sprechen Teil 1) you get a card with these seven keywords and say one sentence for each. Then the examiner asks you to spell your name and say a number. Fill in your details: the text updates with the English meaning. Practise it aloud until you can say it without reading.

Name? · Alter? · Land? · Wohnort? · Sprachen? · Beruf? · Hobby?
Reminder · No article before a job. Ich bin Ingenieurin. (not eine Ingenieurin). The feminine form adds -in: Ingenieur → Ingenieurin, Lehrer → Lehrerin.
Reminder · Countries. Most have no article: aus Brasilien, aus Deutschland, in Hamburg. A few do: aus der Schweiz, aus der Türkei, aus den USA.
Reminder · Languages and hobbies. Languages end in -isch and have a capital letter: Englisch, Portugiesisch, Chinesisch. A verb used as a hobby is a noun, so it gets a capital too: das Lesen, das Kochen. Or say it with gern: Ich koche gern. (I like cooking.)

gern (to like doing)

German has no verb "to like doing". You add gern right after the verb: Ich schwimme (I swim) → Ich schwimme gern (I like swimming).

Small talk starts with the weather, and you hear it in announcements. You only need one pattern: Es + verb or adjective. Tap a picture to hear it.

22 · Hotel und Post (hotel and post office)

Two classic situations: you check in at a hotel and you send a letter or parcel. Both need numbers, names and addresses. Everyone you talk to here is a stranger, so use Sie.

RezeptionGuten Abend. Wie ist Ihr Name, bitte?Good evening. What is your name, please?

GastMüller. M – Ü – L – L – E – R.Spell your name (see lesson 8).

RezeptionJa, ein Einzelzimmer für drei Nächte. Das kostet 70 Euro pro Nacht, mit Frühstück.Yes, a single room for three nights. It costs 70 euros per night, with breakfast.

GastWann gibt es Frühstück?When is breakfast?

RezeptionVon 7 bis 10 Uhr. Hier ist Ihr Schlüssel: Zimmer 12, im ersten Stock.From 7 to 10. Here is your key: room 12, on the first floor.

Auf der Post (at the post office)

Ein Brief (a letter) · where does everything go?
der Briefletterdie Briefmarkestampdas Paketparceldie Postkartepostcarddie Adresseaddressdie Postleitzahlpostcodeder Absendersenderder Empfängerrecipient

KundeIch möchte diesen Brief nach Spanien schicken. Was kostet das?I would like to send this letter to Spain. How much is it?

PostDas kostet 1,85 Euro. Möchten Sie noch eine Briefmarke?That costs 1.85 euros. Would you like a stamp as well?

KundeJa, bitte. Und dieses Paket wiegt zwei Kilo. Wie lange dauert das?Yes, please. And this parcel weighs two kilos. How long does it take?

PostEtwa drei Tage.About three days.

Reminder · Postcode first. In Germany the postcode comes before the town: 80331 München. Say the postcode digit by digit (acht · null · drei · drei · eins).
